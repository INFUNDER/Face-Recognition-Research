Preliminary single-run results on the fixed split (train subjects 1-100, test 101-142) that
motivated the cross-validation study; superseded by `results/hfr/cv` and not used in the paper.
`zeroshot_*` are the pretrained EdgeFace-XS/S models, `xs_*_seed0` fine-tuned EdgeFace-XS
(`mod` = rank-space scale modulation, `mod_anchor` = with the visible scales frozen),
`vis_bench_*` their LFW accuracy.
